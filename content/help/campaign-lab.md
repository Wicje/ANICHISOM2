# Campaign Lab

Campaign Lab is your central command for managing marketing projects, assets, and tasks.

## Command Center
The Command Center gives you a bird's-eye view of all active campaigns. Use the tabs to filter by Active, Negotiating, or Archived projects. Click any project card to open it in detail.

## Audience Hub
Manage your community and audience segments in the Audience Hub. You can view top demographics (age, gender, location) and create custom segments based on user interaction.

## Assets Library
Store all your campaign assets in one place. Drag and drop files to upload them, or import from Google Drive. You can filter by asset type (Image, Video, Design, Copy).

## Managing Tasks
Tasks can be assigned to team members with a specific due date and status. Keep track of progress using the hierarchical task list (Phase > Task > Subtask).
