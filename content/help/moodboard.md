# Moodboard

The Moodboard is an infinite canvas for visual ideation. 

## View Modes
- **Grid Canvas**: Free-form nodes, connection lines, and zooming.
- **Timeline**: View your nodes chronologically.
- **Asset Preview**: Preview your images/text in standard templates (Social, Ad, Email).
- **SamurAI Board**: Generate images from text prompts using AI.

## Adding Content
You can add text, upload local files, or paste URLs. Links to YouTube, Instagram, or images will automatically render as embed nodes.

## Collaboration
All changes on the Moodboard are synced in real-time. You can see other users' cursors when they are viewing the same project. Leave comments and emoji reactions on specific nodes to provide feedback.
